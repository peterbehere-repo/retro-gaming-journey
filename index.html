    Sync

    Your handhelds work without a connection, so their progress rides along on the memory card. Bring the card here — or plug the handheld in — and this page copies it across: achievements, a safety copy of your saves, and what your RetroAchievements account has unlocked.

    1 · Collect achievements

    Every game keeps a note of the achievements you unlock. Connect the handheld whichever way suits you and the page reads what is new, lights up the matching stars, then tells the handheld it can clear the space.

        Technical details for the curious

        On the card, progress is one achievement per line in rgj/rgj-queue.jsonl. After each read the page writes rgj/rgj-ack.json back — that receipt is how the handheld knows which achievements it can clear.

        2 · Back up your save files not connected

        Copies your saves and save states off the handheld into dated backups. Nothing is overwritten — every version is kept, and you can put any of them back or download it.

        Where backups are kept not connected

        Backups are kept in a free private folder on GitHub, which also gives you the full history. Add a GitHub key once and this page can write to it. Your own folder is the only place anything goes.

        Find the key on GitHub under Settings → Developer settings → Personal access tokens. It stays in this browser and is only sent to GitHub.
